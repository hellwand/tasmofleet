You can plan an update for later. The update preview has a "5 · When" card: start now (the default) or at a chosen time, with the quick picks Tonight 03:00, Tomorrow night 03:00 and "Like last time", or any other date and time. The time is read in your browser's time zone (in the server's without JavaScript), a time that is not in the future is refused, the plan shows when the update starts and what happens if TasmoFleet is down then, and the button reads "Schedule update". A planned update waits with its devices shown as "Planned" and starts at that time; the audit log records the planned start. The help of the "Send when" notification setting now says that "problems" also sends a scheduled update that was not started (#604)
